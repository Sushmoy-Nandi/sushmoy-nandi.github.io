## Deep Explainable Federated Learning Framework for Detecting Thyroid Cancer with Feature Selection Techniques

A research prototype combining EfficientNetB0, recursive feature elimination, federated averaging, and Grad-CAM for thyroid ultrasound classification.

The research explores model interpretation and collaborative learning without exchanging raw training images. [View the research poster](https://github.com/Sushmoy-Nandi/Detecting-Thyroid-Cancer-Efficientnetb0/blob/main/Poster_Presentation.png).
