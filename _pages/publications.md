## Thyroid ultrasound research

### Deep Explainable Federated Learning Framework for Detecting Thyroid Cancer with Feature Selection Techniques

This research prototype combines EfficientNetB0, recursive feature elimination, federated averaging, and Grad-CAM for thyroid ultrasound classification. It investigates collaborative model training and interpretable predictions.

[Code and research poster](https://github.com/Sushmoy-Nandi/Detecting-Thyroid-Cancer-Efficientnetb0)
